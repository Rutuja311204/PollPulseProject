What a basic form builder leaves to you

Collecting answers is the easy part. PollPulse helps with the parts before and after.

StageTypical basic formPollPulse
Writing questionsWhatever you type is published.A quality checker flags leading and double-barrelled wording, near-duplicate questions, missing “Other” choices and mixed rating scales before you publish.
Question typesMostly text and multiple choice.Twelve types with their own rules: selection limits, rating and scale ranges, number limits, date windows, email format, and rankings that reject repeated positions.
CollectingOne page, open until you remember to close it.Opening and closing dates, response limits, one-question-at-a-time mode, save and resume, and duplicate protection per browser or per account.
Reading resultsCounts and pie charts.Completion rate, time to complete, averages and distributions, ranking scores, cross-tabulation with a chi-square test, and keyword and tone summaries for written answers.
Trusting the numbersYou work out what a percentage means.Every metric is defined on the page, and the written summary is produced by fixed rules from those numbers.
Working togetherShare an edit link.Owner, editor and viewer roles per survey, plus an admin panel with moderation and an audit log.

Twelve question types, each validated on the server

The browser helps respondents, but the server checks every answer again, so a tampered form cannot store an option that does not exist.

Quality check example. “Don't you agree the library should always stay open late?” — This question may be leading, and “always” makes it hard to answer honestly.

Single and multiple choice

Optional “Other” with text, minimum and maximum selections.

Rating and linear scale

Stars from 1–3 up to 1–10, or numbered scales from 0 or 1 with end labels.

Ranking

Order items by preference; results show average rank and a Borda score.

Number and date

Minimum, maximum and whole-number rules; earliest and latest dates.

Short and long text

Length limits, plus keyword, topic and tone summaries in analytics.

Email, dropdown, yes/no

Format-checked email, long option lists, and quick binary questions.
