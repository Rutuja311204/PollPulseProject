How these numbers are calculated

Responses
Submitted responses in the period (by submission time).
Option %
Respondents who chose the option ÷ respondents who answered that question. For multiple choice the total exceeds 100%.
Completion rate
Visits that ended in a submission ÷ visits that opened the survey page (by start time). Responses sent through the API are not visits.
Abandoned
Visits with no submission and no activity for 30 minutes.
Time
Submission time minus the time the respondent opened the survey; times over 24 hours (resumed drafts) are excluded.
